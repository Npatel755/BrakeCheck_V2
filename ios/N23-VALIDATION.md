# N23 — Steadier danger meter and lane corridor

The danger pill on Camera and Navigation shows only the risk level or brake warning, without checking, buffering, selection or acquisition status. Presentation continuity now holds the last measured level for up to three seconds while a vehicle remains visible or its track remains active. Missing estimates never extend that deadline; confirmed absence clears the meter. A different known target starts fresh. Rising risk is immediate; falling bands still require 350 ms of new observations. A held level does not create numeric estimates, spoken warnings or haptics. Existing five-second triggered brake warnings retain their separate lifetime.

LaneDisplayContinuity smooths small changes in observed lane boundaries and holds the visible corridor for up to three seconds across inference gaps. Original capture times remain intact. Large changes or recovery after a gap use the latest geometry directly. Resetting tracking or changing camera geometry clears the corridor. This helper is used only by DriveModel's overlay; VehicleTracker and lead eligibility keep their actual fresh inference and visual tracking checks.

Validation: all 346 Core tests passed. Added checks cover the three-second deadline, immediate absence, lead gaps, delayed scores, lane smoothing, original timestamps, scene changes, invalid/old inference, expiry and reset. Native release compilation and IPA integrity, revision, executable identity and eleven model-resource checks. Physical checks remain: short detection/lane gaps, empty road, changed lead, GPS interruption, turns and camera rotation.
