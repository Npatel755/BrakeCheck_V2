What should I label? · Quick guide

Painted lines: 2 = left of your lane, 3 = right of your lane. 1 and 4 are the next painted boundaries farther left and right. A double stripe is one boundary; connect dashes along the same line.

Road edges: left and right road limits, even on unmarked roads. Trace only the visible extent. A curb or grass edge is a road edge, not a painted line. Shadows, cars, crosswalks and pavement repair seams are neither.

No painted lines clears only paint in your draft. Road edges may still be present. No boundary here means this side visibly has no continuing edge, such as an open junction. Unclear / hidden means you cannot judge an edge because it is hidden, too dark, or outside the photo. Not reviewed yet leaves that side unfinished.

Save correction accepts your draft. Exclude photo removes the whole image from training; use editing to fix poor proposals. Skip for now makes no decision.

Draw from near to far. Tap to place points; drag handles to adjust. Use Undo last point for a misplaced tap, Undo edit / Redo for other changes, and Pan photo to scroll while editing. Keyboard: Ctrl/⌘ Z, Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z, Backspace, Escape to finish.
