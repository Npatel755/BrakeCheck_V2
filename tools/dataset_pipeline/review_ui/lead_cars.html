Labeling guide

The lead is the nearest car, truck, bus or motorcycle occupying the lane the camera vehicle is following. Box the visible vehicle body tightly; stop at image borders for clipped vehicles. Do not guess a hidden body. When a turn or junction makes lane identity ambiguous in a still photo, skip it.
