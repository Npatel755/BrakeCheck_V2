About BrakeSense

BrakeSense adds a forward-collision warning to cars that don’t have one, using an iPhone mounted on the dashboard. The phone’s camera finds the car ahead, estimates the gap and how fast it’s closing, and says “Brake” out loud when a collision is getting close. Detection runs on the phone, so warnings work without signal, and video stays on the device. BrakeSense also shows turn-by-turn directions on the camera view, recaps each drive, saves clips, and checks in after a hard impact. Collision warnings are free; an optional Premium subscription adds more alerts.

BrakeSense started as a team project at Hacklanta 2026. Harrison and Nick, two students, are building it into an app.

What we ask

BrakeSense is a driver aid, not a safety system, and it hasn’t been independently tested. Please don’t describe it as preventing crashes. The IIHS finding that built-in forward-collision warning cuts rear-end crashes by 27% is about systems built into cars, not about BrakeSense.
