Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026. This policy covers the BrakeSense iPhone app and this website.

The short version

  • BrakeSense works on your iPhone. The camera feed, car detection, distance estimates, trips, video and clips stay on your phone.
  • The app has no ads, analytics or trackers, and we don’t sell or share your data.
  • Our server is used only if you add emergency contacts. It stores their first names and phone numbers and, during a crash check-in, your location and speed so it can message them.
  • You can delete all of it: in the app, by deleting the app, or by emailing us.

Who we are

BrakeSense is made by Harrison and Nick, two independent developers. “We” and “us” in this policy means them. You can reach us at support@brakesense.app.

What stays on your iPhone

These are handled on the phone and are not sent to us:

  • Camera. During a trip, BrakeSense uses the rear camera to find vehicles and estimate how far away they are. Frames are processed in the phone’s memory.
  • Video. While a trip runs, the app keeps the last 15 minutes of video and deletes older footage automatically. When you tap Save clip, or the app detects a hard jolt, it keeps the 15 minutes before and after. Saved clips stay until you delete them. The video has no sound.
  • Location and speed. Used to show your speed, give directions and save trip routes. Trip history and routes are stored on the phone.
  • Motion. Used to notice a possible impact.
  • Microphone. After a possible impact, BrakeSense listens for a short spoken answer, such as “I’m okay.” Apple’s on-device speech recognition turns it into text on the phone. The audio isn’t saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings, recent searches and saved places. Stored on the phone.

Apple services the app uses

  • Apple Maps. When you search for a place or ask for directions, your search and location go to Apple to return results. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.
  • App Store purchases. Premium is bought through your Apple Account. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card details. The app asks Apple whether your subscription is active.
  • Spoken alerts. The “Brake” alert and spoken directions use the iPhone’s built-in voices.

Emergency contacts (optional)

This is the one feature that uses our server, and only once you add a contact.

  • We store each contact’s first name and phone number.
  • When a crash check-in starts, the app sends our server the time, your location if the phone has a recent GPS fix, your speed and your language. Your answer (“I’m okay” or “Send help”) follows.
  • If you ask for help, or don’t answer within about a minute, our server messages your contacts with your location. Messages are delivered by our messaging provider, Photon, as an iMessage or text.
  • If you send a test message to a contact, it goes only to that contact.

We keep contact details until you remove them in the app or ask us to delete them. We delete check-in records, including location, 30 days after the check-in, unless you ask us to keep them longer, for example for an insurance claim.

Only add people who have agreed to receive these messages.

This website

  • The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.
  • Our web host keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser type, page requested) for a short time to keep the site secure.
  • If you join the waitlist, your email address is stored by our form provider. We use it once, to tell you BrakeSense is on the App Store, and delete the list after that email goes out. Ask us any time and we’ll remove you sooner.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent personal data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Your video never leaves your phone unless you share a clip yourself.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete saved clips from the Clips screen and saved trip routes in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Remove emergency contacts in the app, or email us to delete everything on our server.
  • Turn permissions on or off in iPhone Settings › BrakeSense. Collision warnings need the camera, and speed needs location.
  • Depending on where you live, including California and the EU or UK, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

For people in the EU and UK: we rely on your consent for emergency contacts and check-ins, on our contract with you for Premium, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure for request logs.

Children

BrakeSense is meant for licensed drivers. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

The app talks to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only the people who run BrakeSense can access the server. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep as little as we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change affects data you’ve already given us, we’ll ask you first.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@brakesense.app.
