Support

Email support@brakesense.app and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

Getting set up

  1. Mount your phone so the rear camera faces the road and sees the car ahead. Portrait and landscape both work.
  2. Open BrakeSense and tap Start trip. A short check confirms the phone is mounted, the camera and location are allowed, and the volume is up.
  3. Drive. Leave the phone alone; the alerts and directions come to you.

Something isn’t working

The meter says “No reading”

BrakeSense can’t see a car clearly in your lane. Clean the windshield and the camera lens, check the phone isn’t tilted toward the sky or the dashboard, and give it a moment after glare or a tunnel. It also reads less well at night.

“Camera unavailable”

The camera is turned off for BrakeSense in iPhone Settings › BrakeSense › Camera, or another app is using it. Close the other app and try again.

Speed shows dashes

Speed comes from GPS. Allow location in iPhone Settings › BrakeSense › Location, and wait a few seconds for a fix, especially after a garage or tunnel.

I don’t hear the “Brake” alert

Turn the volume up, or connect the phone to your car’s speakers over Bluetooth. Spoken alerts play through whatever audio output the phone is using.

Directions won’t load

Map search and directions need an internet connection. Collision warnings keep working without one.

The phone gets hot

Camera, GPS and screen together create heat, and a sunny dashboard adds more. Use a vent or lower mount out of direct sun, and charge with a cable rather than wirelessly if you can.

Premium

  • Restore a purchase: on the Home screen, tap Explore Premium, then Restore purchases.
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › BrakeSense › Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: Apple handles these. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Delete saved clips from the Clips screen, and saved trip routes in Settings.
  • Remove an emergency contact on the Emergency contacts screen.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To delete what’s on our server (emergency contacts and check-in records), email us from any address and tell us which phone numbers to remove.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
