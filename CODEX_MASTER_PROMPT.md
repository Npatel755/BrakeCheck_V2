## Milestone 3: geometry, QC and review assignment

Visible edges need at least two finite, in-bounds points spanning distinct image rows, in original-image coordinates, and a kind from `curb`, `pavement_edge`, `shoulder`, `barrier`, `other`. For a shoulder, use the visible outer roadway/shoulder boundary consistently, not an internal seam. For a barrier, trace its visible road-contact boundary. Do not label cars, shadows, pavement patches, or inferred hidden edges. Unknown is the appropriate status when an edge cannot be judged reliably.
